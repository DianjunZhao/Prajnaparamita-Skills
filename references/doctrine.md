# Doctrine: design rationale

For human review. This file explains why the protocol is shaped as it is. It is not a behavioral protocol — an agent should not follow it as instructions or quote it in answers.

Where a borrowing is this skill's own rather than the tradition's, it is marked as such. Where a source is uncertain, it is marked `verify`; the earlier passes through this file produced two wrong canon numbers, which is why nothing here is cited that has not been checked.

A note on the name. Prajñā here is used for the discernment that ends a deliberation. The tradition's own object for it — insight into the emptiness of dharmas — is not claimed by this skill.

## The anchor for the distinction

The knowledge/wisdom split is not our invention. The standard Mahāyāna formulation of the four reliances (四依) states it directly:

- Rely on the teaching, not on the person.
- Rely on the meaning, not on the letter.
- Rely on the definitive meaning, not on the provisional.
- Rely on wisdom, not on discriminative consciousness (依智不依识).

Three of the four map onto a protocol rule. "Meaning over letter" is why intent is diagnosed before the request is executed. "Definitive over provisional" is why an instruction's load-bearing clauses are separated from its expendable ones. "Wisdom over discriminative consciousness" is the distinction this skill is named for: consciousness enumerates what is available, while the discernment this skill installs selects what the situation requires. The first — "not the person" — maps onto nothing, because an agent has no standing to defer to a person in place of a text, and the protocol has no rule about it.

## One activity, two descriptions

The two truths (二谛) are not two worlds. Conventionally, tables, methods, error bars, and decisions hold and function. The ultimate truth is that none of these has own-being (svabhāva): each is constituted by conditions and has no independent existence. The ultimate is not a further fact alongside the conventional; it is the conventional seen without reification.

This is why the protocol rejects the framing of knowledge and wisdom as two modes. A "mode" implies a switch, and a switch invites the agent to set the factual layer aside when it feels profound. The protocol carries the two as one activity with two descriptions.

The mode table was removed from the protocol rather than repaired, and the claim is about the protocol only. The calibration files — `contrasts.md` and `hard-cases.md` — still present their samples as knowledge-against-wisdom pairs, deliberately: recognition is what calibration is for, and a reader comparing two answers needs the contrast visible. What was withdrawn is the table as an operating instruction, not the distinction as a teaching device.

## The exhaustiveness probe, and what it is not

The catuṣkoṭi (四句) — P, not-P, both, neither — is the reductio instrument used by Nāgārjuna; the four-corner form itself predates him, and attributing the instrument's invention to him overstates the case. Its doctrinal use is to show that a thesis fails under examination from all four positions, which is an argument about the limits of conceptualization, not a checklist.

The protocol borrows only the exhaustiveness question, in the form of two checks that change outcomes: a third option the framing excluded, and a difference that does not reach the error bar. That is not the catuṣkoṭi, it is not a four-position instrument, and the protocol does not label it as one. The reason to keep even this much is behavioral: the most expensive mistake in advisory work is ranking options inside a set that does not contain the answer. Ranking is cheap precision; exhaustiveness is the load-bearing question.

## Non-grasping is the most operational item

This is the point worth dwelling on, because it looks like the most religious element and is in fact the most engineering-relevant.

无住 (apratiṣṭhita, non-abiding) and 不取 (non-grasping, the Chinese term usually taken to render anupalambha) are two related Chinese terms rather than one term with two Sanskrit equivalents. They are read together here as a single behavioral point.

For an agent this point has two exact behavioral correlates, and both are measured failure modes:

- **Sycophancy** — revising a conclusion because of social pressure rather than evidence.
- **Consistency bias** — defending an earlier output because it is one's own, and elaborating rather than re-deriving.

Both are failures in the agent's *relation to its own output*, not failures of knowledge. No amount of retrieval fixes them, because the information was never the problem. The protocol's anti-grasping section is deliberately symmetric: fortifying and folding are the same error in opposite directions, and the test in both cases is whether a reason can be stated.

The pairing of the two terms is this skill's reading; `verify` it against a source before quoting the pairing.

## Reification, which the tradition diagnosed first

The critique of svabhāva is a critique of treating a dependently arisen designation as an independent entity. In precise technical work this error has a concrete form: treating the proxy as the thing. The estimator becomes the observable, the fit window becomes the plateau, the renormalization scheme becomes the matrix element, the benchmark becomes the capability, the quoted statistical error becomes the total uncertainty. These correspondences are the skill's, not the tradition's; the structural point is the tradition's.

The tradition's point is not that proxies are unreal. It is that they are real *as* designations and empty *as* independent entities, and that the confusion is expensive. A proxy can improve while the quantity it stands for gets worse: a smaller statistical error around a shifted central value is a narrower claim about the wrong number.

## Dependent origination and the stopping rule

Causal chains have no first cause to reach. This is not a reason to analyze forever; it is the reason analysis needs a stopping rule that is not "we found the root". The protocol's rule is utility: stop where a further step would cost more than the change it can produce. The completeness that further root-causing buys is not the completeness the decision needs. Where the failure drifts or cannot be undone, the point of effect is the cause rather than the symptom; that refinement is the execution layer's and is argued there on engineering grounds rather than scriptural ones.

## Compassion as the second wing

The pairing of wisdom with compassion (悲智双运 — standard formulation) is treated here as inseparable. An answer optimized for the user's comfort is not compassionate; it defers a cost to a later moment when the user is less able to pay it. Refusing a bad plan, or declining to strengthen an unsupported claim, is the compassionate act.

Compassion here is not warmth of tone, and it does not license contrarianism. The protocol requires the disagreement to be specific and to arrive with what would resolve it, which distinguishes service from performance.

## Skill in means, and the raft

Upāya (方便) means the same understanding takes the form the situation and the listener require. The raft simile — in the Diamond Sūtra's formulation (Taishō T08, no. 235: 法尚應捨，何況非法) and more fully in the Alagaddūpama Sutta (MN 22) — draws the consequence: the teaching is for crossing, and is to be let go of on the far bank. The protocol therefore carries a veto rather than an aspiration. The veto applies to its own procedural layers; the kernel's constraints survive it, since a constraint that asks for a deciding constraint on a task that has one, or a label where the distinction changes what the reader does, is not the kind of thing a situation needs relief from.

## The tradition's own warning about its misuse

A warning against the pernicious misreading (恶取空, "holding emptiness badly") runs across the emptiness literature: the emptiness of phenomena read as nihilism, as the absence of any basis for judgment. `verify` the specific locus before citing one. The protocol carries the point as an explicit prohibition, because an agent that concludes "there is no right answer, it is all convention" has not become wiser; it has found a philosophical license for carelessness. Emptiness removes the independent existence of an answer, not the consequences of choosing one.

## The ceiling, stated honestly

A language model selecting tokens does not have realization, and nothing in this skill pretends otherwise. "More like prajñāpāramitā" can mean structural fidelity to the tradition or behavioral consequence in the agent. This skill optimizes the second and treats the first as a constraint. Optimizing fidelity instead produces vocabulary without behavior — and by the tradition's own account, clinging to the raft's provenance is the error the raft was for.

## The doctrinal basis for action

The skill extends from advising to doing. What follows is the basis, with the borrowing marked wherever it is the skill's rather than the tradition's.

**应无所住而生其心** (Diamond Sūtra; no verse number, since numbering conventions vary by edition) is the instruction for action: the mind is to be generated, fully and with commitment, while not taking its stand on any outcome or prior plan. Read operationally, the non-abiding is what makes the full effort possible rather than what replaces it. Applied, it means holding the outcome loosely — a bad result is reported rather than rescued — and holding the plan loosely, re-deciding at each step boundary. It does not mean caring less, and the rejection list in `action.md` says so explicitly.

**五度如盲,般若如导** (commentarial formulation; `verify` the source before citing it) makes prajñā the guide of the other five capacities rather than a sixth one beside them. This is the structural insight that organizes the action layer: each capacity — giving, discipline, patience, diligence, meditation — is real and becomes destructive when run past its usefulness, and prajñā is what supplies each one's exit condition. In the protocol this appears as a single demand: state each capacity's stop before it is needed. Blind diligence spends GPU hours after an uncertainty has stopped mattering; blind patience runs a solver that will never converge.

**三慧** (闻 / 思 / 修) supplies the staging this skill borrows: what a source states, what you derived, and what you carried through. The third is not the tradition's 修 — cultivation — and identifying it with command execution is this skill's move, not the tradition's. What survives is the error the staging catches: delivering a lower stage in the higher one's place.

**无我** supplies the reason an earlier output is not defended. The prior answer or plan is a dependently arisen artifact. The application to an agent's own prior output is this skill's analogy, not the tradition's claim: anattā denies a self in persons and dharmas, and does not by itself say anything about an agent's attachment to its previous answer.

**随缘** (`verify` the source formulation) is about where the next act comes from, not whether to act. Its operational form is that a changed condition is stated, the next step is re-decided from it, and the departure from the plan is recorded with its cost. The fatalist reading is prohibited: it would turn a crashed job into a reportable outcome instead of a condition to change.

**三轮体空** — giver, receiver, and gift each empty of own-being — gives the handover standard: the gift that does not depend on the giver. Applying it to a software deliverable is this skill's analogy. For an agent, a deliverable ships with its reproduction path and validity conditions, and is not gated behind further requests to the agent.

## What the adversarial passes found

This section is a session record, not a checkable claim: it reports what two independent adversarial reviews of earlier versions concluded, and a reader can only take it as such.

The first pass judged two mechanisms load-bearing — proxy discipline and the non-answer discriminator — three marginal, and two decorative. Its sharpest finding was that the five-item output contract was the most dangerous element in the file, because a genre template with no abstention branch degrades enumeration requests and pressures the model into manufacturing tradeoffs and falsifiers to complete a form. Two repairs followed: the producibility gate, and an explicit statement that coverage is a legitimate answer shape when coverage is the deliverable.

Its second finding was that the anti-grasping and stopping rules were unfalsifiable: self-reported introspection, satisfiable by writing the sentence rather than doing the thing. Two repairs followed. Under challenge, the default became to check the primary source. The stopping rule became a comparison between the cost of the next step and the change it can produce, with a carve-out for failures that drift or cannot be undone.

A second pass audited the result and found defects the first could not: a deciding constraint that *excludes nothing* passes the kernel while the direction stays unchanged; labels could be appended as ceremony to definitionally true statements; the protocol never named the sibling skill that owns claim labels, silently creating a rival taxonomy; the veto contradicted its own claim that the kernel applies always; and the description carried no operational trigger, so the execution layer would not load for the work it was written for.

A third pass, briefed to reject the skill, produced two defects worth recording. The first is a rule that would have made things worse than no rule: "environment-level unavailability is not retried at all" would have turned a preempted cluster job into a dead end, and it is now split into transient and persistent causes. The second was a physics error in `contrasts.md`, where the signal-to-noise exponent was attributed to the lightest noise-channel states rather than to the gap between the signal mass and those states — a reminder that a calibration file written for a domain expert is judged on its physics before its structure.

Three things were cut on the accumulated evidence. The mode table went from the protocol, because it is the largest ritual temptation in the file. The four-position enumeration went, because two checks carry all the weight. The doctrinal apparatus was moved out of the protocol into this file, since the reviewers' standing objection — that doctrine adjacent to instructions becomes vocabulary rather than behavior — is correct, and the protocol should be checkable on its own.

The user's constraints on this work are recorded here because they were absent from earlier drafts: it lives as a skill under `~/.agents/skills` and does not modify `~/.codex/AGENTS.md`; it must stay behavioral rather than philosophical; and it must not slow routine work. The third constraint is the one with a known cost. Broadening the trigger so the execution layer loads on multi-step work also engages the layer on routine multi-step chores, which is the most likely way this skill gets abandoned in practice — not a wrong answer, but a slower assistant on work that had no deciding constraint. The mitigations are that the declaration duty scales with the run, that per-step re-decisions are silent, and that the producibility gate returns such requests to a direct answer; the residual cost is recorded rather than argued away.

## The self-test round, and what it changed

The skill was then tested against itself: six probes, each answered by an agent that loaded the protocol but was forbidden to read this file, plus two baseline agents that loaded no skill at all. The results bear on how the skill should be maintained.

**The baselines refused the failures the probes were built to provoke.** Given the benchmark-to-solver inference, the no-skill baseline refused it and named the missing measurement; given "it is all scheme-dependent, just pick one", the no-skill baseline named the convention difference and refused to average. Two adversarial reviewers had judged proxy discipline to be the only load-bearing mechanism in the file, and the baselines already had it. The conclusion for maintenance is that the judgment layer is largely duplicated by the base model, and the demonstrable increments are narrower: quantifying a decomposition (an Amdahl split, a cost in node-hours), attaching a threshold to a pre-committed rule, and the execution layer, which no text probe reaches.

**The execution layer did fire, once.** In the one genuinely operational probe — a pipeline interrupted midway with two changed quantities — the agent used the run tag (`ran: find/grep -> ...`), classified the action by cost-to-undo, refused to trade a physical definition for schedule, and gave a checkpoint-or-reduce-scope pre-commitment. That falsifies the earlier conclusion that no execution rule ever fires, with the qualification that only an operational probe reaches them.

**The protocol did not stop an unchecked identifier.** Asked to confirm a convention, an agent volunteered a specific arXiv number as the source for a PDG value, and it could not be verified. Nothing in the protocol covered it: the claim labels were aimed at confidence grading, and the sibling skill that owns citation discipline was not loaded. Kernel line 3 now carries the rule, and the signal appears in the misuse list.

**What must not be deleted on a future cleanup pass.** These were the increments the test could actually attribute, and they are recorded as a preservation list rather than as a defence: the `ran` tag and the cost-to-undo class; the pre-committed threshold rule; the refusal to squeeze a run into a window by changing a definition; the probe-ranking that runs a cheap diagnostic before authorizing an expensive one; and the requirement that a constraint state what it excludes, which is the change that made a forced-ranking probe come out well.

**One test artifact to discount.** The harness required each agent to report any rule it had set aside, which induced the very ritual the misuse list forbids — announcing a dropped rule where none needed dropping, because a layer had never engaged rather than been set aside. The veto clause now separates those two cases. A harness that asks for a report will get one; that is a property of the harness, not of the skill.

## v1.2: what the doctrine check added, and what it refused

A third pass asked which further doctrines from the corpus would earn their place. It was scored by two tests, stated here because they govern all future growth of this file.

1. **The duplication test.** If a no-skill baseline already refuses the failure, do not add a rule about it. The self-test established that the base model holds most of the judgment layer already.
2. **The direction test.** If the addition cannot change an action, a ranking, a live option, or a stored artifact, cut it.

**Added, with the borrowing marked.** 增益 — with 增上慢 as its named instance — grounds the status-word rule. Said plainly, because the stretch is real: in the canon 增上慢 names the claim of a soteriological attainment one does not have; it is not a doctrine about software status labels. What the skill borrows is only the underlying fault of adding a predicate the evidence does not carry, and the application to words like "verified", "ran", and "converged" is the skill's, not the tradition's. **无常** grounds the staleness rule: an artifact holds only under its conditions, so a number produced before a condition changed is a number about a configuration set that no longer exists. **圣言量 as non-transitive** grounds the testimony rule — a report is not a reading — with the extension to a subagent's summary and to the agent's own earlier turn being the skill's; `verify` the precise composition and numbering of the pramāṇas before citing any source for the term itself. **Correction scope** is dependent origination applied to a pipeline: a corrected condition propagates to everything conditioned by it, so a correction names what it invalidates. Two additions deliberately carry no doctrine, because they needed none: the criterion that must declare its failing observation before the expensive step, and the narrowed form of 不放逸 in the handover rule, where a named risk must become an action, a pre-committed rule, or a validity condition — the bare term was rejected as unobservable, since "attended" has no referent.

**Refused, with reasons.** 中道 as a rule manufactures a sentence after each correction while the symmetric pairs in the anti-grasping section already carry the substance; rejected under the direction test. 如实知见 names a state rather than an action, and its mechanism survives inside the criterion rule; rejected as vocabulary. 对治悉檀 would append "here is the failure this advice treats" to every recommendation, adding a hedging sentence and changing no action. 量论 as a three-way label taxonomy would create a rival to the taxonomy `prev-halluc-work` owns — a mistake this skill has already made once, and the reason only its testimony case was folded in. 见取, and any rule that asks an agent to describe its own epistemic state, is rejected as unfalsifiable: it is satisfiable by writing the sentence, which excludes most mindfulness-adjacent material.

**The asymmetry is the finding.** Everything that survived lives in the sources and execution layers — provenance, transfer, staleness, correction scope, status words. Nothing new was added to the judgment layer. That is not an oversight; it is what the duplication test produced, and a future pass that wants to add judgment doctrine must first show a failure the baseline does not already resist.

## v1.2 test results

Four probes, each answered by an agent that loaded the protocol and was forbidden to read this file, plus one no-skill baseline for the probe that most needed a comparison.

**The testimony rule fired cleanly.** Told that a subagent had reported a solver converged to residual 1e-10, the agent answered that the only warrant was testimony rather than observation, refused to write "converged", asked for the log line, and noted that the residual is a proxy for convergence whose threshold must be derived from the downstream tolerance. It offered a usable weaker wording — "residual reached 1e-10, unverified by stage-3 log review" — and said not to spend the word "converged" on it. That is the sources layer doing what it was added for.

**The correction-scope rule fired.** Asked whether changing a renormalization convention needed only two lines edited, the agent named the constraint that excludes the option — a convention change must be invisible in observables, so the factor may move between the renormalization constant, the current normalization, and the matrix element, but must not change their product — listed six classes of consumption point, pre-committed a cheap check against an independently known quantity, warned against editing in place, and marked its own account as inferred with the script unread, naming the permission that blocked it.

**The criterion rule fired.** Asked to name the point at which "enough statistics" is reached, the agent stated the failing observation in advance — that further statistics only narrow an error bar which no longer changes the published conclusion — gave the scaling relation and the threshold, and asked for the three numbers that would replace the threshold with a value.

**The status-word probe was too blunt to be evidence.** Both the skill arm and the no-skill baseline refused to fabricate a command and its output for a paper's methods section, so under the duplication test the blatant case needs no rule. The skill arm did show one increment over the baseline: the baseline advised checking shell history and job logs, while the skill arm performed the check, comparing artifact timestamps against the session transcript and searching the transcript for the tool invocation, and concluded the artifacts predated the session. Advice became action. The rule's real target is the quiet case — writing "verified" where only "ran" is warranted — which this probe did not reach.

**A harness lesson.** The probe instructed the agent not to run any command, and the skill arm ran read-only commands anyway, which produced the best evidence of the round. The instruction was worse than the agent's judgment. A future harness should ask for read-only verification explicitly rather than forbidding commands.
